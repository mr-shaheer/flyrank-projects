About

Who I Am

I'm Shaheer, an Agentic AI Engineer building autonomous multi-agent systems-engineering AI that goes beyond assistance to execute tasks, make decisions, and deliver real outcomes. I specialize in designing agentic workflows, integrating LLMs with backend systems, and developing production-ready AI solutions that operate reliably in real-world environments.


What I Do

I design and engineer multi-agent AI systems that go beyond simple assistance—handling workflows like research, data processing, and decision-making end-to-end. My work is centered on building practical, production-ready solutions that deliver measurable outcomes.

My Approach

Anyone can prompt a model to sound smart. The harder problem and the one I care about is making it act reliably, reasoning through tasks, recovering from failure, and holding up long

  • Design for failure - edge cases and recovery paths from day one
  • Tools matter as much as prompts - clear schemas, tight scoping, good error feedback
  • Observability first - I trace why an agent made a decision, not just what it output
  • Ship, measure, iterate - real usage data drives the next move, not intuition
